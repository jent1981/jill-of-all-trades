Our Story Started by women who knew there was a better way

We Didn't Break the Mold.
We Built a Better One.

Jill of All Trades was born in 2018 when our founder — a licensed general contractor with 15 years of experience — grew tired of watching Oklahoma homeowners get overcharged, underserved, and talked down to. She knew women could do the work better, and she set out to prove it.

What started as a one-woman operation in Oklahoma City grew into a full-service team of skilled tradeswomen. Each brings expertise, professionalism, and genuine love of the craft. Today we're proud to be one of Oklahoma's most respected home improvement companies — and we're just getting started.

  • 100% female-owned and operated since day one
  • All-female crew of licensed, trained tradeswomen
  • Fully insured and licensed throughout Oklahoma
  • Serving residential and light commercial clients
  • Proudly serving seniors and women in our community
  • Free estimates on every project, always
